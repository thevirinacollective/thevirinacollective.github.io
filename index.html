European interdisciplinary research collective

Understanding the conditions that affect women

The Virina Collective brings together researchers across Europe who study different aspects of women's health, from the microbiome and the immune system to pain, psychology and nutrition. Together, we aim to build a systemic perspective and advance research in the field.

Our focus

Many conditions that affect women are painful and under-researched. We bring different fields together to build a systemic perspective.
