## Who we are

The Virina Collective is a European research network on women's health. It is coordinated by Hind Hussein and Laura Díaz, who are responsible for the personal data described on this page. You can reach us about anything related to your data at [thevirinacollective@gmail.com](mailto:thevirinacollective@gmail.com).

## What we collect

**When you join the network** through our form, we collect:

- your name and email address, and a second email address if you give one
- your institution or organisation and country
- your career stage and field(s) of expertise
- anything you choose to tell us about your work
- your consent, and the date you gave it

**When you email us**, we keep your message and email address so we can reply.

Giving us your details is voluntary. We need them to add you to the network and keep you informed, so we cannot add you without them.

## Why we collect it, and on what basis

We use your details only to keep you informed about the network, our news and our events, including our first conference.

We do this on the basis of your consent, which you give when you submit the form (Article 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation). You can withdraw your consent at any time, and this does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

We do not use your details for advertising, we do not sell them, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

## Where your data is stored

Your details are stored in The Virina Collective's Google account, in Google Forms, Google Sheets and Google Contacts, provided by Google Ireland Limited. Google may store or process this data outside the European Union, including in the United States. These transfers are covered by the safeguards that EU data protection law requires: the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Who can see it

Only the members of the board of The Virina Collective can access your details. We do not share them with anyone outside the board, unless the law requires us to.

## How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as you are part of the network. If you ask to leave, we delete them within one month. Once a year we review the list and remove entries that are clearly out of date, such as email addresses that no longer work.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- see the data we hold about you and receive a copy of it
- correct it if it is wrong or incomplete
- receive the data you gave us in a common, machine-readable format, or have it sent to someone else
- have it deleted and be removed from the network
- withdraw your consent at any time
- ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it

To use any of these rights, write to [thevirinacollective@gmail.com](mailto:thevirinacollective@gmail.com). We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work, for example the CNIL in France or the Data Protection Authority in Belgium.

## Expert interviews

If you take part in one of our expert interviews, we ask for your consent separately before the interview and explain then how the recording and transcript are stored and used. We send you the edited transcript to proofread, and we will not use the interview for anything other than informing our work without asking you again.

## This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. The website itself does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not load anything from other websites.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If the change matters to how we use your details, we will also let members of the network know by email.
