Conditions that affect women are usually studied one field at a time. To build a systemic perspective, we first want to understand where each field stands, and that starts with talking to the experts.

## What we are doing

We are conducting structured interviews with experts across our fields of focus: the microbiome, immunology, psychology and neuroimaging, pain, and nutrition. The aim is to map the most pressing knowledge gaps and research priorities in women's health.

## Who we are looking for

Researchers and clinicians with experience in one of these fields, or in another area relevant to women's health.

## What it involves

- One recorded online interview of about 60 minutes.
- We use the recording to prepare an edited transcript, which we send to you to proofread.
- For now, the interviews only serve to inform our work. If we decide to use them for anything else, we will contact you again and will not go ahead without your consent.

## What happens next

These insights will help shape the programme of our first conference, which will bring experts together to discuss the future direction of the field and lay the groundwork for collaborative European research.
