Our focus

Women's health, from a systemic perspective

Conditions that affect women rarely sit within one organ or one discipline. They involve the immune system, the nervous system, the gut microbiome, psychology and nutrition. We bring together researchers working on these different aspects to share knowledge across fields and build a systemic perspective.

Conditions within our focus include

Endometriosis

Tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside it, often causing chronic pelvic pain.

Premenstrual disorders

Including PMDD: mood and physical symptoms in the days before menstruation that disrupt daily life.

Vulvodynia

Chronic vulvar pain without an identifiable cause, often left undiagnosed for years.

Irritable bowel syndrome

Chronic abdominal pain and altered bowel habits, with symptoms that often shift across the menstrual cycle.

Other conditions

Other conditions that affect women, and broader questions of sex-specific physiology.

Why it matters

Common, painful and under-researched

Women's health remains critically underrepresented in biomedical research. Existing studies are largely epidemiological or clinical and rarely look at underlying mechanisms, so many of these conditions still lack effective treatment. Many also share a pain component whose mechanisms remain poorly understood.

Hind Hussein
